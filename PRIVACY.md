# Privacy

Adwi Clock is meant to feel calm, including in how it treats your data.

## What the app accesses

The app asks for foreground location permission so its colors and background
can match the weather around you. If permission is granted, it reads a device
location at launch and refreshes the weather theme about every 20 minutes
while the app is open.

The latitude and longitude are sent directly from the device to the
[Open-Meteo weather API](https://open-meteo.com/). Please see
[Open-Meteo's privacy and usage terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) for
their current data-handling details.

## What the app does not do

Adwi Clock has:

- no account or sign-in;
- no advertising;
- no analytics or tracking SDK;
- no location history stored by the app;
- no server operated by this project; and
- no microphone or background-location access.

If location permission is denied or weather cannot be loaded, the app simply
uses a time-of-day color theme. The stopwatch and timer continue to work.

This notice describes the open-source app in this repository. Forks or
modified distributions may behave differently and should publish their own
privacy notice.
