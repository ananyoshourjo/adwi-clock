# Code of conduct

Adwi Clock is a love letter disguised as a timer. Its community should feel
just as gentle.

Be kind, be patient, and assume good intent. Feedback should be specific to the
work, never cruel to the person doing it. Harassment, discrimination,
sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, and other behavior that makes people
feel unsafe are not welcome here.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and conversations that break
these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. If something feels wrong, contact a maintainer privately rather
than escalating it in public.

We want beginners to feel safe asking questions and experienced contributors
to feel comfortable saying “I don't know yet.” Everyone deserves that room.
